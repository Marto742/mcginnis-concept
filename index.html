Our story

Built in Gastonia, since 2015

With encouragement from Ben and Stephanie Causby of Causby Karate Academy, Shawn McGinnis opened McGinnis Academy of Martial Arts in 2015. After retiring from the Gaston County Police in 2016, he began teaching full time — bringing real-world experience into every class, from Little Ninjas to adult self defense.

FAQ

Before your first class

Do I need any experience?

No. Most students start as complete beginners, and every program begins with a free trial class.

How old does my child need to be?

Kids classes start at age 5. Our Little Ninjas class is for ages 5–6.

What should I wear?

Comfortable workout clothes for your first class. We'll talk about uniforms if you decide to join.

Is the women's self-defense class beginner-friendly?

Yes. It's built for people with no martial arts background.
